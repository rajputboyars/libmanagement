StudyQuest roadmap: pehle Library Admin Dashboard

Idea simple hai. Pehle library owner ka dashboard banao, jahan se woh poori library chala sake. Phir priority ke hisaab se ek-ek feature usme jodte jao. Har step ke neeche simple Hinglish me likha hai ki woh kya hai, owner ko kya fayda hai, aur kaise kaam karta hai.

1. Pehle zaroori kaamSeat, fees, attendance. Bina iske library chalti hi nahi.
2. Phir control aur time ki bachatReports, reminders, staff. Owner ka kaam kam hota hai.
3. Phir library badhanaNaye students, rewards, branches, paise kamana.
